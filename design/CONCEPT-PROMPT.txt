Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: brand and mobile game concept board for Dicey!
Primary request: Create a polished landscape art-direction board for a bright, gamey multiplayer dice game named exactly "Dicey!". It must feel like a charming party videogame with expressive original creature avatars and tactile game pieces.
Composition: warm cream board with a big original chunky tilted Dicey! wordmark at upper left, six expressive circular creature avatar portraits below it (coral fox, turquoise frog, purple cyclops, yellow bird, blue bear, pink cat), a compact row of colour swatches, and two large readable mobile screen concepts on the right. First phone is a home screen with the wordmark, a playful cluster of dice and avatars, large "Play together" and "Challenge friends" buttons. Second phone is an active turn with two player avatar badges labelled "Ben" and "Mia", "Ben's turn", "Round 4 of 13", five dice in a roomy tray with two visibly held dice, "2 rolls left", a big "Roll dice" button and a short score-preview area with "Full House 25" and "Chance 18". Use functional readable hierarchy, few words, broad touch targets.
Style: premium illustrated casual game, flat graphic shapes mixed with restrained soft toy-like depth, thick midnight outlines, offset shadows, plump rounded corners, slightly wonky lettering, expressive faces. Bright tangerine coral, sunshine yellow, turquoise, grape purple, warm cream and midnight ink. Dice pips remain crisp. Energetic but uncluttered. Avatars recognizable at tiny sizes.
Constraints: no dashboard, no sidebar, no charts, no admin panels, no corporate visual style, no gambling/casino imagery, no photorealism, no existing IP or brand characters. This is a visual concept rather than a production screenshot.
